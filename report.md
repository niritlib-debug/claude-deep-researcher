## 4. Salary ranges, senior QA in Tel Aviv

The sources disagree, and all are self‑reported or modelled estimates.
- **ERI SalaryExpert:** a senior level qa engineer (8+ years of experience) earns an average salary of ₪ 233,790 a year, about ₪19.5K a month. Its overall range is between ILS 143,957 and ILS 250,431.
- **Glassdoor, QA Automation in Tel Aviv:** about ₪17K–27K a month for the 25th–75th percentile, with top earners have reported making up to ₪31,000 (90th percentile). Glassdoor's pages label these figures "per year", but its own range text says "/mo", so they are monthly.
- **Glassdoor, QA Engineer (general):** ₪12K-₪20K/mo.
- **PayScale, all of Israel:** A mid-career Quality Assurance (QA) Engineer with 5-9 years of experience earns an average total compensation of ₪216,000. That figure rests on only 7 salaries.

**My estimate:** a senior QA with strong automation skills is most likely to earn about **₪22K–30K gross a month** in Tel Aviv high‑tech. Manual‑heavy roles sit lower, around ₪15K–20K. Leads and automation architects can go above ₪30K, and equity varies by company. This is an inference from the sources above, not a published figure. No posting I found listed a salary.

## 5. Most in‑demand skills (from the postings I read)

1. **Playwright, plus Python/pytest or TypeScript.** Seemplicity requires it, and one Glassdoor snippet mentions Hands-on experience with automation frameworks such as Playwright.
2. **AI‑assisted testing.** This now appears in almost every posting (Cursor, Copilot, Claude Code). DriveNets wants someone to direct AI agents for test generation and triage.
3. **API testing** (REST, GraphQL, gRPC), **SQL**, and **CI/CD** (GitHub Actions, Jenkins).
4. **Docker/Kubernetes, Linux and cloud** (AWS/Azure).
5. **Formal test documents (STP/STD/STR) and owning test strategy.** Israeli postings still treat these as must‑haves, which plays well to 15 years of experience.
6. **Cybersecurity domain experience.** Several roles name it as preferred, which is a real advantage given Checkmarx and Transmit Security.
