## 3. Salary ranges (be careful with these)
- **Glassdoor, Senior QA Engineer, Israel:** the typical range is between ₪18,917 (25th percentile) and ₪28,333 (75th percentile), and top earners have reported making up to ₪32,867 (90th percentile). That is based on 155 salaries … as of September 2025. Glassdoor labels these figures "per year," but they are clearly monthly gross amounts.
- **Glassdoor, QA Automation Engineer, Tel Aviv:** between ₪16,521 (25th percentile) and ₪27,396 (75th percentile), with a 90th percentile of ₪31,000.
- **ERI SalaryExpert (employer surveys):** a senior level qa engineer (8+ years of experience) earns an average salary of ₪ 233,790 a year, roughly ₪19.5K a month.
- **PayScale:** reports a mid-career Quality Assurance (QA) Engineer with 5-9 years of experience earns an average total compensation of ₪216,000 based on 7 salaries. That sample is too small to rely on.

**My estimate:** about ₪22K–30K gross a month for a senior individual contributor with your experience. Lead or architect roles, and well-funded security companies, likely pay above that. This is my reading of the data, not a published figure.

## 4. Most in-demand skills (from the postings above)
1. **Playwright.** It appears in almost every posting, ahead of Selenium and Cypress.
2. **Python with Pytest, or TypeScript.** Real coding ability is expected, not just scripting.
3. **CI/CD.** Mostly GitHub Actions and Jenkins.
4. **API testing.**
5. **Cloud (AWS, GCP, Azure), Docker and Kubernetes.**
6. **AI-assisted testing.** Cursor, Copilot, Claude Code, or building LLM-based testing agents is now listed by Claroty, Island, Unframe, TAG, Akeyless, Dream and VAST.
7. **Networking and security knowledge**, for the cyber and infrastructure roles.
8. **Building automation frameworks from scratch**, which senior roles treat as the dividing line.

Built In's listings show the same pattern; one hybrid senior Tel Aviv role lists Github Actions, Jenkins, Linux, macOS, Playwright, Pytest, Python, Windows.

## Confidence & gaps
- **Postings:** these are high confidence as of 16 Oct, but I read them on LinkedIn and Built In rather than on each company's own careers page. Check the company page before applying. Built In's "reposted" dates hide the original posting date. Work model (hybrid or office) was not stated for most LinkedIn roles.
- **What I didn't cover:** Hebrew job boards (AllJobs, Drushim) and agency listings such as Ethosia and Gotfriends, which carry many senior manual and E2E roles. I also didn't check the careers pages of your former employers (Transmit Security, Checkmarx, Kaltura) for a possible return.
- **Market mix:** pure manual or E2E senior roles are rare. Almost every senior posting expects hands-on coding.
- **Salary:** low to medium confidence. Glassdoor's data is self-reported, mislabeled as annual, and from 2025. ERI's figure is an estimate built from surveys. I found no recent Israeli salary survey (for example from Ethosia or Gotfriends) to check these against.
